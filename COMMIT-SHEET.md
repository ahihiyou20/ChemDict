# COMMIT-SHEET: ChemDict, a Vietnamese–English chemistry dictionary

Committed before any UI code. Register: **`system`** (a multi-route reference app: home instrument, entry pages, lesson pages, with a shared token set and a full-text index). The `system` register runs Phases 1, 2, 4, 6 and 7. One element is borrowed from Phase 5: a single GSAP timeline for the wow peak, plus a ScrollTrigger reveal.

Vinh reviewed the register on 2026-10-24 and kept `system`, then asked for a tasteful 3D scene on the opening view (not needed during lookups). That request is recorded as an amendment in §7, which borrows the Phase 5.3 WebGPU/TSL recipe and the applicable Phase 3 controls.

## 0. Content contract

- **Single source:** Google Sheet *"[Hóa ESL 11A10 + 11E2] Bảng từ vựng"*. Tabs read: Bài 10 (15 terms), Bài 11 (15), Bài 12 (0, empty tab), Bài 13 (13). That is 43 senses, which merge into 40 headwords because *Organic compound* and *Molecular formula* appear in more than one lesson.
- Terms, IPA, part of speech, both definitions, illustrations and source citations are copied verbatim. Whitespace is the only thing normalised. The only text this build adds is alt text that describes each picture.
- Every sense shows its own citation (the sheet's "Nguồn tham khảo" column, linked with tracking parameters stripped). **Vinh, 2026-10-24:** the site never names the vocabulary sheet, shows no sheet/tab/row provenance, and credits senses without a citation (all of Bài 13) to "Internet". The ledger section is titled "Một số nguồn tham khảo" and the footer keeps only the `/` hint and the theme toggle.
- Data is hardcoded in `shared/data/lexicon.json`, generated by `scripts/import_sheet.py` from an `.xlsx` export, so Bài 13 can be re-imported later. There is no database, following the brief. That overrides the Drizzle default in CLAUDE.md, which applies only when there is data to store.

## 1. Recon notes (Phase 1)

- Context7 and the Awwwards MCP are **not reachable from this cloud session** (context7.com and docs.google.com return 403 through the network proxy). The Constrain and Triangulate steps were done against installed type definitions and library source instead. Playwright runs as a library through `scripts/shoot.mjs` and `scripts/motionqa.mjs`, the same engine the Playwright MCP drives.
  - `@orama/orama@3.1.18`: `create({ schema })`, `insertMultiple`, `search(db, { term, properties, boost, tolerance, limit })`. The English tokenizer's splitter `/[^A-Za-zàèéìòóù0-9_'-]+/` drops Vietnamese diacritics, so every indexed field is **pre-folded** (NFD, strip marks, đ→d). Searching without diacritics is therefore a feature.
  - `gsap@3.15.0`: `SplitText.create({ type, mask: 'lines', autoSplit, onSplit })`, `gsap.matchMedia()`, `ScrollTrigger`. All plugins ship in the free package.
  - `nuxt@4.6.2`: `app/` + `shared/` layout, `nuxt generate` for static prerender.
  - `three@0.186.1`: `WebGPURenderer` from `three/webgpu` with `await renderer.init()`, `backend.isWebGPUBackend`, `renderer.onDeviceLost`; `PointsNodeMaterial` on a `Sprite` with `count` (instanced point sprites); `instancedBufferAttribute<'vec3'>`, `uniform`, `mix`, `smoothstep`, `time` from `three/tsl`.
- Font coverage was audited with fontTools against the actual glyphs the data needs: Vietnamese, IPA (ɔ ɒ ɜ ʊ ʃ ʒ ˈ ˌ ː θ) and subscript ₂. No candidate serif (Newsreader, Literata, Gentium subset, Source Serif 4, Fraunces, Spectral) covers the IPA set. **Noto Sans Mono** covers both Vietnamese and IPA fully, so IPA is set in the mono family as an instrument readout. Subscript digits are rendered as `<sub>` so no glyph falls back.

## 2. Grammar: **live-surface**

The hero *is* the instrument from pixel zero: a search plate sitting on a **mass-spectrum-style chart**. Every one of the 43 senses is a vertical line on an axis grouped by lesson (B10 · B11 · B12 · B13). As you type, Orama scores raise matching lines into peaks. The top peak is annotated with its term, and the next two get relative-intensity numbers, the same way the textbook's neon spectrum labels its peaks (90,9 / 0,3 / 8,8). The copy orbits that surface.

### Feeling curve (home)

1. **Instrument:** curiosity turning into agency. A scan line sweeps the axis once on load and the comb of 43 lines rises behind it. After that, every keystroke reshapes the peaks.
2. **A–Z index:** calm orientation. A hard ground cut to the elevated surface brings dense two-column typographic lists grouped by initial letter, which can be sorted in English or Vietnamese.
3. **Lesson lanes:** recognition, since students find their own lesson. Lanes are revealed by a clip-path wipe rising bottom-to-top like a solvent front, which happens once.
4. **Source ledger:** trust. A plain table with no motion: the sheet, the citations and their counts.

### Entry page

The headword is revealed by a line-mask, then senses are numbered as real dictionary senses (a genuine sequence, so `NO_FAKE_SECTION_NUMBERS` is satisfied). Each sense shows the VI and EN definitions as a bilingual pair, an illustration plate where one exists, and a citation line. The page closes on the next-entry plate.

## 4. Tokens

### 6-role OKLCH palette

The palette is drawn from phenolphthalein and chromatography paper. Light is the default, with dark mode via `prefers-color-scheme`.

| Role | Light | Dark |
|------|-------|------|
| Ground primary | `oklch(0.975 0.006 215)`, cool paper (H 215, outside the beige band) | `oklch(0.18 0.015 235)` |
| Ground elevated | `oklch(0.945 0.011 215)` | `oklch(0.225 0.018 235)` |
| Text primary | `oklch(0.24 0.025 250)` | `oklch(0.95 0.008 215)` |
| Text secondary | `oklch(0.46 0.03 235)`, ink-tinted | `oklch(0.76 0.025 220)` |
| Brand accent | `oklch(0.53 0.2 355)`, phenolphthalein pink | `oklch(0.76 0.15 355)` |
| Hairline | `oklch(0.87 0.014 220)` | `oklch(0.33 0.02 235)` |

All text and accent pairs are verified at **≥ 4.5:1** in Phase 7, which computes them in the browser.

### Typography floor (2 families)

- **Newsreader Variable** (opsz + wght) for headwords, body and definitions. It has full Vietnamese coverage.
- **Noto Sans Mono Variable** for IPA, labels, axis ticks and citations. It has full Vietnamese and IPA coverage.
- Display headword: `clamp(2.6rem, 6.4vw, 6rem)`, ≤ 6rem, letter-spacing −0.025em. Hero H1: `clamp(2.25rem, 5vw, 4.25rem)`.
- Prose measure 65ch (definitions), line-height 1.55 for body and 1.05 for display.
- Read top to bottom, the headlines tell the story: *Từ điển thuật ngữ Hóa học Anh – Việt*, then *Mọi mục từ, xếp theo vần*, then *Theo từng bài học*, then *Một nguồn duy nhất*.

### Spacing

4px base: `--s1` 4px up to `--s24` 96px. Section padding is `clamp(56px, 9vw, 128px)` on top and `clamp(40px, 6vw, 88px)` on the bottom, so there is always more space above a heading than below it.

### 5 depth tools

1. **Offset shadow:** the search plate uses a hard 1px under-rule plus a soft `0 18px 30px -22px` ink shadow.
2. **1px edge highlight:** the search plate and lesson cells get an inset top highlight `inset 0 1px 0 oklch(1 0 0 / .6)`.
3. **Scale and blur distance plane:** the spectrum gridlines sit on a back plane at lower contrast, and peaks sit in front.
4. **Structural overlap:** on desktop the illustration plate hangs past the sense column into the margin, and the lesson matrix overlaps the hero top edge.
5. **Film grain:** a static SVG noise layer on the ground at 3% opacity (5% in dark). It is never animated.

### Motion budget

- UI: hover and press at 140ms, peak height changes at 220ms with `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`, focus rings instant.
- Choreographed: an opening scan of 1.6s (one timeline, once), a headword line mask of 0.9s with a 0.06 stagger, and a lane solvent-front wipe of 0.9s (once).
- **No Lenis:** this is a reference tool. Ctrl+F, anchor jumps and the `/` focus need native, instant scroll, so the single-ticker contract is moot. GSAP's own ticker is the only rAF loop.
- Reduced motion: no scan, no mask and no wipe. Peaks update instantly. The layout and the comb stay intact.
- **Transitions over 300ms, with reasons:** the 3D cloud's canvas settles in over 600ms (opacity + scale 0.96 → 1) and its caption fades over 600ms. Both are part of the choreographed opening, not a UI state change; dimming the cloud for a lookup takes 250ms.

## 5. Named anti-references (banned in this build)

1. A floating, glowing 3D ball-and-stick molecule over a dark navy-to-purple gradient hero.
2. Bubbling flask or beaker icons and emoji, and a neon "toxic green" lab palette.
3. Periodic-table tile wallpaper or hexagonal benzene background patterns used as decoration. The lesson matrix uses the element-cell form **once**, as functional navigation.

## 6. Architecture (Phase 4)

- `shared/types/lexicon.ts`, `shared/utils/lexicon.ts` (fold, slug, merge senses into entries, IPA and subscript formatting) and `shared/data/lexicon.json`. There are no Vue or Nitro imports in `shared/`.
- `app/` holds pages `index.vue`, `tu/[slug].vue` and `bai/[n].vue`, the components, and `composables/useLexiconSearch.ts`, where Orama is built lazily on the client.
- `nuxt generate` builds fully prerendered static HTML. Every entry and lesson route is listed explicitly for prerender. JS is off-critical-path: without JS all content and links render, and only the live search needs JS (a `<noscript>` note points to the A–Z index).
- Deploy: Nitro preset pinned to `static`, so every host serves the same `.output/public`. `vercel.json` (build, output, cache and security headers) and `public/_headers` (the same headers for Cloudflare Pages). `.nvmrc` pins Node 22.

## 7. Amendment: the opening 3D scene (requested by Vinh)

**What:** a procedural electron-density cloud of **benzene, C₆H₆**: σ density around the six carbons, six hydrogens and their bonds in ink, and the delocalised π system as two rings above and below the plane in the phenolphthalein accent. It is drawn as about 9,500 instanced point sprites (about 6,700 on the WebGL2 fallback), from a seeded PRNG so every visit draws the same molecule. It turns slowly (0.12 rad/s), the π rings breathe along the ring normal, and a fine pointer tilts it slightly.

**Why benzene:** it is a real entry in the source sheet (*Aromatic compound*, Bài 10). The caption names it as a model, "Đám mây electron π của benzene, C₆H₆ (mô phỏng)", and links to that entry, so the decoration is also a way into the dictionary.

**Placement:**
- Desktop: right column of the hero, beside the title, with its caption.
- ≤ 860px: an absolute backdrop behind the display title at 55% opacity, masked out before the lede, so it never pushes the search down or sits behind body copy.
- During a lookup: fades out in 250ms and stops rendering. The instrument owns the screen.
- No JS, or no WebGPU/WebGL2: the figure is removed and the hero stays typographic.

**Anti-reference check:**
- #1 bans a glowing ball-and-stick over a navy-to-purple gradient. This is a dot-density cloud on the paper ground, with no glow, bloom or gradient, in the two committed hues.
- #3 bans hexagonal benzene patterns as wallpaper. This is one captioned molecule that links to its entry, not a repeated motif.

**Feeling curve:** the cloud fades in during the opening moment, at the same time as the scan, and stays ambient. The scan remains the single wow peak.

**Engineering contract (Phase 5.3):**
- `three/webgpu` + `three/tsl` only, with `await renderer.init()` before any render.
- Exactly one canvas on the page; geometry, material and renderer are disposed on unmount.
- Loaded with `requestIdleCallback` (timeout 1.5s) and never preloaded.
- **Off the main thread.** three.js (~240 KB gzip) is parsed and drawn in a module worker on an `OffscreenCanvas`. Evaluating it on the main thread was a ~490ms long task. The scene module is DOM-free: theme colours are resolved on the page and posted to the worker.
- **Main-thread fallback.** Where a worker cannot draw, a fresh canvas takes over and the same module runs on the main thread.
- GSAP's ticker is still the only render loop. It posts one frame message per tick, with backpressure, so a slow worker merges frames instead of queueing them.
- The loop pauses when the figure is off-screen (IntersectionObserver) and while the reader scrolls (ScrollTrigger `scrollStart` / `scrollEnd`).
- Pixel ratio: ≤ 2 on WebGPU, ≤ 1.5 on WebGL2, 1 on software GPUs.
- `onDeviceLost` removes the figure instead of leaving a dead canvas.

**Performance tiers (Phase 3 "quality tier", automatic, no HUD):**
- `animated`: the default.
- `still`: one art-directed frame, drawn when:
  - `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` is set;
  - the GPU is CPU-emulated (SwiftShader, llvmpipe, WARP, fallback adapters);
  - the median frame time of the first ten steady frames, on the page or in the worker, exceeds 22ms.
- `unavailable`: the figure is removed.

The tier is exposed as `data-tier` for QA.

**Other Phase 3 items for this register:**
- An auto/light/dark theme toggle, persisted per reader and applied before first paint.
- No sound and no preloader. The content is prerendered HTML and the scene has no layout dependency, so CLS is unaffected.

### Lookup-speed contract (Vinh: animation must never slow a lookup)

- Entry content is never pre-hidden. Only decorations carry `data-pre`.
- No route transition covers or delays a page. The band sweep runs on the indicator strip.
- The 3D scene builds only after a quiet moment: page idle, no lookup active and no key pressed for 1.2s. A lookup that starts mid-build terminates the worker; the scene is rebuilt when the field is clear. Its first frame can hold the GPU process (and with it every page frame) for hundreds of milliseconds on a slow or software GPU, which is exactly when a reader types.
- Shaders compile through `compileAsync` (parallel where the driver allows). Point sprites draw without MSAA. A software GPU gets one still at 0.75× resolution with 9k points, drawn once and never while the stage is hidden.
- While a lookup is active the stage fades out and then leaves the compositor (`visibility: hidden`).
- Motion scopes are killed, not reverted, on unmount: their DOM is discarded anyway, so the next page mounts without that layout work.
- Phase 7 gates it: Enter → answer on screen < 150ms, and the answer is visible at load.

  - benzene → *Aromatic compound*
  - butane → *Organic compound*
  - formula table → *Molecular formula*
  - neon spectrum → *Mass spectrometry*
- **Linked pictures:** column I links 25 Bài 11 terms to pictures on other sites (BBC, Pinterest, GeeksforGeeks and others). This build does not copy them. The site draws its own apparatus instead: eight animated line diagrams (separation, distillation, evaporation and condensation, extraction with a separatory funnel, solution, crystallization, filtration, column chromatography). Each term lights the part of the apparatus it names. Nothing claims they are the linked pictures.

### Concept: the reader's scroll is the solvent

Bài 11 is now the bulk of the dictionary, and it is about separating a mixture. So the home page below the hero becomes **one continuous piece of glassware**, run by the reader's scroll.

The **spine** is a glass tube in the left gutter, from the hero's foot to the footer. Its liquid level follows the reader down the page (`scaleY`, scrubbed, `ease: 'none'`). Every section is a stage of the apparatus, and each stage starts only when the liquid reaches its tap:

| Stage | Apparatus | Reaction it triggers |
|---|---|---|
| Hero → marquee | **Condenser.** The marquee runs inside a cooling jacket. Vapour from the hero condenses into droplets that slide with scroll velocity. | Droplets fall into the spine. |
| A–Z index | **Manifold.** One side-arm per letter group. | When the liquid reaches a side-arm, the arm fills and its outlined initial crystallises (fill). The fill runs from letter to letter like a fuse. |
| Lesson lanes | **Column chromatography.** One dark band of mixture enters the column and separates into the four flame colours as the reader scrolls. Each colour runs at its own rate, which is what separation is. | Each colour leaves the column into its lesson's strip, and the strip develops: the solvent front crosses it and the term spots come up. |
| Source ledger | **Burette.** The tube is graduated, and drops fall. | Each source row receives drops, and its abundance bar fills to its share of the 88 senses, read as a mass-spectrum abundance. |
| Footer | **Endpoint.** | The last drop turns the receiving flask phenolphthalein pink (the brand accent), and the footer rule turns pink. |

**Ambient ground.** In the right gutter, the hero's electron cloud keeps rising as vapour: small flame-coloured points and bubbles drift up at three depths. Their rate follows scroll velocity, like heat: idle is a slow drift, and a fast scroll boils.

**Coupling.** Pointing at anything that belongs to a lesson lights every member of that lesson across the page and dims the rest. That covers the header cell, spectrum lines, marquee separators, A–Z dots, strip spots, ledger rows and spine taps. It is one attribute on `<html>` plus CSS, so there is no per-element script.

### Where things may sit (Vinh: never over text)

- Apparatus lives only in the side gutters, in section padding, and inside its own figure boxes.
- Text columns never have art behind them. QA checks that no decorative element's box intersects a text node's box.
- Below 860px the gutters are 16px. There:
  - The spine thins to a 3px liquid line at the edge.
  - The taps become 8px stubs.
  - The vapour field is off.
  - The column, burette and condenser keep their in-section figure boxes.

### Scene change

- **View Transitions**, through Nuxt `experimental.viewTransition`.
- **Effect:** the old page is wiped downwards by a solvent front, a band in the four flame colours. The new page is already underneath, so the headword is uncovered first, within about two frames of the transition starting.
- **Timing:** 420ms on `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`, a short-lived clip-path.
- **Header:** the header and the indicator strip keep their own transition names, so they hold still.
- **Off:** under reduced motion and where the API is missing. The indicator-strip sweep stays as the fallback.
- Phase 7 gates it: Enter → answer uncovered < 150ms.

### Engineering contract

- The spine, taps, column, burette and vapour animate only `transform` and `opacity` on composited layers. The one exception is the scene change's short-lived clip-path.
- All of it is created after the opening and the 3D's quiet start.
- It stops while a lookup is active or the tab is hidden.
- Each stage runs only while its section is on screen (IntersectionObserver or ScrollTrigger toggles).
- Under reduced motion, every stage is shown in its final state: full spine, developed strips, filled bars, pink endpoint. Nothing moves.
- Without JS, the same final state comes from markup and CSS.
- No canvas is added. The page keeps at most one canvas, the 3D hero.
- The vapour field is DOM points: about 18 at 1440px wide.

### As built (2026-10-24)

What shipped, and where it departs from the plan above:

- **One front line.** Every stage reacts when its anchor crosses 62% of the viewport (`FRONT_LINE`, `app/lib/train.ts`). The liquid's leading edge, an indicator-pink dot, rides exactly there, so the reader always sees where the reaction is.
- **The spine starts at the marquee, not the hero.** The marquee's terms span the full width, so glass from the hero would cross them. The marquee is the condenser: teal coolant dashes run along both walls against the terms (always rightwards, faster when the band is thrown), and the train's glass leaves from its outlet at the bottom-left, dripping while the band is on screen.
- **A–Z.** No side-arms: the index's columns leave no clean taps. Terms come out as soon as their group is on screen (they are content); each outlined initial fills with flame when its group reaches the front line.
- **The column** sits in the left gutter at the A–Z/lessons seam. All four bands load as one dark mixture; each travels down at its own pace and leaves the outlet exactly when its own fraction's pipe starts, so the column shows a real separation while it is on screen (Bài 10 elutes first, Bài 13 last).
- **The chart recorder** (new) runs down the right gutter beside the strips: one Gaussian peak per lesson, level with its feed, height from its number of terms. The paper is uncovered down to the front line by counter-moving frame and paper (transform only), and a pen rides the signal there.
- **The burette** sits beside the ledger head. One drop per ledger row (the total row is the last drop); each drop that lands fills its row's bar and carries the titration curve on. The curve (new, in the ledger head) is a real strong acid / strong base curve whose volume steps are the sources' shares of the senses, so the sources together reach the equivalence point and the total tips it over. Past pH 8.2 the line turns pink, every bar flushes pink with the flask, and the footer rule turns pink. Rows that never climb to the front line before the page ends have their windows squeezed into the scroll that is left, so the endpoint always arrives at the foot of the page.
- **Ambient ground.** Instead of a page-wide vapour field, the pot boils in the free right gutter beside the A–Z: 22 DOM bubbles nucleate, grow and burst, faster while the reader scrolls, paused off screen. The loop reads: the pot boils on the right, the vapour crosses the condenser right to left, the condensate runs down the left.
- **Coupling** is `data-focus` on the home root (`useLessonFocus`), not on `<html>`, so a hover restyles the home page only. Sources: strips, spectrum columns and ticks, ledger tags, index terms, the title's flame bands. Effects: other strips and spectrum columns step back to 32%, same-lesson index terms step aside for their lesson dot, ledger rows of that lesson tint, that lesson's pipes and column band glow, other recorder peaks fade.
- **Scene change** is `app/plugins/scene-change.client.ts`, not Nuxt's experimental flag: a 440ms flame-front wipe in a view transition, or a flame-front sweep over the new page where captures would cost the lookup (no API, software GPU, a capture slower than 50ms, or a "/" jump straight into typing). Measured on the cloud's SwiftShader: sweep 96ms Enter → answer, forced wipe 288ms; the gate measures what a reader actually gets.
- **Entry pages** draw the eight apparatus plates (`LabDiagram.vue`) for the 25 Bài 11 senses whose pictures live on other sites. The part a term names (the receiver for *Distillate*, the vapour for *Evaporation*, the cold watch glass for *Condensation*, the funnel, the extract layer, solvent, solute, stationary and mobile phase) is drawn in pink and labelled with the term.
- **Plate motion** is a function of time (`app/lib/lab-motion.ts`), after the Lemo-Opuscar method: `frame(t)` returns every moving part's pose with no state between frames, applied by transform and opacity on GSAP's ticker while the plate is on screen. The server paints `frame(0)` through a CSS variable, so the first frame is already the motion's first. Nothing appears from nowhere: bubbles nucleate on the hot glass, drops gather at a tip or on the cold glass, dissolved particles leave a crystal face; anything that leaves goes into liquid (drawn over the drop, with a ripple), through its surface or into a crystal. Nothing passes through glass: particles are held inside the funnel's and the paper cone's walls at their height. One-off processes (extracting, dissolving, crystallizing, filtering, eluting, sorting) play once and then keep only what keeps running; going off screen rewinds them.
- **Without JS** the train is absent (its geometry is measured from the laid-out page); every section shows its finished state from markup.
- **Recon.** awwwards.com, tympanus.net and dev.to are unreachable from the build environment, so the references are titles only: "Connect sections by drawing dashed SVG paths on scroll", "Title scroll on SVG path" (New York City Times), "Heading scroll fill" (Gamecan), the Bright Biotech scrolling story (SOTD), and page transitions by Boom Bloom, Digitalists and Cyd Stumpel. What this build takes from them is the principle (one drawn line connecting sections, transitions that uncover rather than cover), not any look.
